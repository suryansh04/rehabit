Ankur, our little sprout

Recovery is a habit, and habits grow. Ankur lives in a small clay pot and grows a new leaf on each day of the streak. He cheers between taps, stays calm while a coin glows, and never looks sad.

Character

A chubby round sprout with two leaf arms and a leaf crown. Big kind eyes, peach cheeks, a terracotta pot with a white dotted rangoli band. Warm and gentle for adults, not a kids' cartoon.

Rules

Calm while a coin glows

Big moves only between targets, so reaction time stays fair. While a coin glows he only glances at it.

Never sad, never scolds

A miss gets a tilt and a thumbs up. A low mood gets a hug and a warm glow, never a hop.

Gentle motion

200 to 800 ms moves, no flashing, small sizes. Everything stops with reduced motion.
